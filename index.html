Hedge Fund Bot

Hedge Fund Bot is a personal, single-user research tool. It runs on a private server owned by its developer and is not offered to the public.

What it does

The tool reads market-related newsletters and summaries that its owner has filed under a Gmail label named "Trading", so they can be used as one input among many in the owner's own investment research. It requests read-only access (the gmail.readonly scope) to the owner's own Gmail account and never sends, deletes or modifies any email.

Who can use it

Only its owner. No other person is asked to sign in, and no data from any other person is collected.
